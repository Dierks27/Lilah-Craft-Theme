How you earn them

Log in every day

Your streak pays more each day in a row: 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, then 5 a day.

Play

A token for every hour you play.

Quests

Three daily jobs and two weekly ones, different for everyone, like catching fish or visiting new biomes.

Achievements

Big moments pay once, like your first Mini or a seven-day streak.

Wild Minis

Catching one counts toward quests and achievements.

Card trade-in

Swap spare Cards for 3 to 50 tokens each. Rarer Cards give more.

Skill games pay for your time

  • Cabinets, courses and mini golf are free. They pay about a token a minute of first-time play, up to 60 tokens a day between them.
  • Today’s pick pays 5 extra tokens for your first finish of it each day.
  • A course’s first finish ever and Weekly Cup prizes come on top of the daily limit.

Days roll over at midnight Central time, so streaks, daily quests and daily limits reset at the same moment for everyone.

        Turn on JavaScript to see this week’s courses, their star times, records and Cups.

        Stars and the Star Chart

        Finishing a course gives 1 star, a good time 2 and a great one 3: each course shows its times. Golf gives 3 stars at par or better.

        Your best stars on each course add up on the week’s Star Chart: 6 stars pay 5 tokens and 12 stars pay 10.

        First finishes

        Your first finish of each course in a set pays 10 to 25 tokens: 120 for the whole set.

        Starting a time trial asks Warm up (3:00) or Go straight to the timed run. A Dropper starts straight away.

        Course codes

        Every course has a code, like HARD-40, on its tile and its screen. Tell an admin the code of one you loved: they can bring it back for a week as a Classic.

        Records and boards

        Each set starts with empty boards. A course’s record and its top five are this set’s; a Classic keeps its old ones.

        The Weekly Cup

        • A course with a Cup shows it above. Enter on the course’s screen in game: 10 tokens, once per course per week.
        • Your Cup time is your best counted run on that course after you entered. Party races count; warm-ups don’t.
        • The pool is every entry, plus 20 tokens from the server once two or more set a time. It pays out when the week turns, Monday at 4:00 AM Central time: 70% and 30% between two Cup times, 50%, 30% and 20% among three or more.
        • With fewer than two Cup times your entry comes back. /hcm play cup lists your Cups this week.

        The Clubhouse

        A room in the Games world where every course and golf round starts and ends. It’s free, and nothing in it costs or pays tokens. Drop in any time with /hcm play clubhouse or the Clubhouse tile on the Arcade’s Play row.

        On the way to a course

        Tap Start on a course and you go through the Clubhouse: a 5-second countdown, then the start line. Go now skips the wait; Stay here keeps you in the room. After the run you’re back, with your result and Play again.

        The wall boards

        Results, live during a race. This week’s best: each course’s fastest run, the Weekly Cup leaders and the records. Now playing: who is where. My stats: only you see yours. On Bedrock the boards float as name tags.

        The trophy case

        One frame for each all-time record: a gold ingot for a course, a golf ball for a golf course. Set a record and the whole room gets NEW RECORD! and a firework.

        The jukebox

        Tap the jukebox on the little pink stage and pick one of 22 records. It plays for everyone in the room, and Quiet turns it off. Nobody gets the disc.

        Family week

        Points for playing: 1 for a finished run, 2 for a personal best, 3 for the week’s best, 5 for a record, at most 10 a day. The week turns at midnight on Monday, Central time, with the podium for last week’s top three when two of them are in the room. Points are never tokens.

        Cabinets inside

        The cabinets play right in the room, from the Games item. Games of chance, crates, the Scratch Ticket and Card Packs are played outside.

        After 30 minutes with no race or party going you’re sent home, with a warning a minute before. Just before a restart nobody new gets in.

        Playing together

        All of it is free and just for fun.

        Race with friends

        Tap Race with friends on a course’s screen, or type /hcm play race <course>. Up to 8 racers and one shared start; the host starts it. Your finish still counts as a normal run, Weekly Cup time included. Never on a Dropper.

        Challenge someone

        Pick a course, then anyone online who is free: a race of two. Find it on a course’s screen, or as Challenge in the Clubhouse.

        Golf together

        Tap Play with friends on a golf course’s screen: up to 4 players on the same hole, each with their own ball. Once one ball is in, the rest have 2 minutes to finish the hole (longer on a long hole of Golf of the Week).

        The Play? screen

        Every invite opens a small screen with Not now and Yes, on Java and Bedrock. It lasts 60 seconds. /hcm play accept and /hcm play deny work too, and /hcm play invites off turns every invite off.

        Friend games

        Connect Four and Tic-Tac-Toe against a friend: no tokens and no high score, just the game.

        Watch and cheer

        /hcm play watch flies you round a race or golf group in spectator mode; the racers can’t see you. /hcm play cheer cheers them on, once every 10 seconds, and /hcm play cheers off stops cheers reaching you.

        Ride along

        A boat driver can take one friend in the back seat with /hcm play rider <player>. The rider isn’t timed or paid.

        How to play

        /hcm play

        The Games screen: every game that’s open right now, by tab.

        /hcm play <id>

        Opens one game: its rules first, and for a game of chance its odds. Each game on this page shows its command.

        /hcm play fresh_courses

        The Fresh Courses that are up now, with your stars.

        /hcm play trials /hcm play golf

        The time-trial courses (this week’s Fresh Courses first, then the rest easiest first) and the golf courses.

        /hcm arcade

        The Arcade, in rows: your Wallet, Play, the Prize Counter, Luck (crates, the Scratch Ticket, Card Packs, Take a break) and You (quests, achievements, My Looks, Give a present).

        /hcm arcade odds

        One line per open game of chance: what it gives back.

        /hcm play break

        Take a break: your own daily token limit for games of chance, or a pause of 1, 7 or 30 days.

        /hcm play clubhouse

        Go to the Clubhouse.

        /hcm leave

        Leave a course, a golf round or the Clubhouse. You go home with all your things.

        The server restarts at 4:00 AM and 4:00 PM Central time. In the 5 minutes before each, no new run, golf round, card hand or scored daily try starts; quick games keep going.

        The fairness promises

        Games of chance give back less than you put in. These rules keep them honest about it.

        Tokens only

        No game takes or pays dollars, or takes your Cards, Minis or anything you could sell. Tokens never turn into money.

        Less back than you put in

        Every game of chance gives back between 85 and 95 of every 100 tokens over many plays. Each one’s own number is on its card, rounded down. Play for fun, not to get more tokens.

        Odds first

        Before your first play, the screen shows what every result pays, how often it comes up and what the game gives back. /hcm arcade odds and this page show the same numbers.

        Decided first, then shown

        The result is saved and paid before any animation starts, and each card is saved before you see it. What stops on screen is what came up. Closing early just shows the result in chat.

        Daily limits

        Every game of chance has its own plays a day. The lowest limit that applies counts, and it all resets at midnight Central time.

        Together they take at most 100 tokens a day, crates, Scratch Tickets and Card Packs bought with tokens included.

        “No win this time.”

        That is all a loss says: no teasing line, no play-again button, no win sound. Getting your own tokens back is never called a win.

        Take a break

        Choose how many tokens go into games of chance each day, or stop them for a while. It works even while the games are closed.

        • Your own daily limit: 10, 25, 50 or 100 tokens, or none.
        • A pause of 1, 7 or 30 days. It ends at midnight Central time and can’t be cancelled.
        • A lower limit starts now. A higher limit, or none, waits 7 days. A pause can be made longer, never shorter.
        • It covers crates, Scratch Tickets and Card Packs bought with tokens too.
        • On request, staff can set a limit or a pause a player can’t lift, or switch games of chance off for one player.

        Open it with /hcm play break or the blue bed in your Wallet.

        Crates and Cards

        Crate

        15 tokens

        One prize per crate, never a hat. Before you open one, the crate screen shows every prize and its chance.

        On average a crate holds less than 15 tokens’ worth at Prize Counter prices. The spin is only a show: your prize is decided before it starts.

        Rare Card

        150 tokens

        Once a week, on the Prize Counter’s Minis tab. Always Rare, Epic or Legendary, and the rarer ones come up less often. If none are left, nothing is charged.

        Card trade-in

        Free

        Spare Cards for tokens: Common 3, Uncommon 5, Rare 12, Epic 25 and Legendary 50 each. Prize Counter, Minis tab, Trade In Cards.

        Take a break covers crates. It doesn’t cover the Rare Card.

        The Prize Counter

        Pick exactly what you want, at a fixed price, on the same tabs as in game.

        Turn on JavaScript to see the prizes and their prices.

        Rented or kept

        Hats and caps last 90 days, trails from Fireworks & Trails 30. From a week before the end until 30 days after, Buy again renews one at the same price. Nothing renews by itself.

        Everything else is kept for good: collectors (a year in the name, sold only in season, one each), Keepers, trophies, golf balls, perks, launchers and the Firework Remote. Boosts and shows are used up when you use them.

        My Looks

        /hcm looks, the Wallet or the Arcade’s You row: every hat, cap and trail you have, with its end date. One tap wears it or switches it on, and a rented hat you lost is made again for free.

        A switch there hides other players’ trails and parties, just for you.

        Give a present

        On the Fireworks & Trails and Looks tabs: buy a collector, a rented look or a golf ball for someone who’s online. It goes straight on them and can’t be undone. Never a Keeper, a perk, a trophy or a boost.

        Prizes are safe

        A prize you carry stays with you when you die. A placed hat or trophy can only be broken by whoever placed it. Place an Arcade Trophy and tap it to open the Arcade.

        Prizes can’t be sold for money: shops, Pallets, Vending Machines, the Auction House and Courier runs all say no. In the trade window (/trade) a prize swaps item for item, never with money in the trade. Keepers and rented looks can’t be traded at all.
