Tokens buy fun

Tokens are the Arcade’s coins. You can’t buy them with money and they never turn into money. You earn them by playing.

How you earn them

Log in every day

Your streak pays more each day in a row: 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, then 5 a day.

Play

A token for every hour you play.

Quests

Three daily jobs and two weekly ones, different for everyone, like catching fish or visiting new biomes.

Achievements

Big moments pay once, like your first Mini or a seven-day streak.

Wild Minis

Catching one counts toward quests and achievements.

Card trade-in

Swap spare Cards for 3 to 50 tokens each. Rarer Cards give more.

Skill games pay a few tokens too: milestones, daily challenges, Today’s pick and first finishes. By default they pay up to 6 tokens a day between them, plus first finishes.

Days roll over at midnight, server time, so streaks, daily quests and daily limits reset at the same moment for everyone.

    How to play

    /hcm play

    The Games screen: every game that’s open right now.

    /hcm play <id>

    Opens one game: its rules first, and for a game of chance its odds. Each game above shows its command.

    /hcm play trials /hcm play golf

    The time-trial courses, easiest first, and the golf courses.

    /hcm arcade

    The Arcade: your Wallet, crates, the Scratch Ticket, the Prize Counter, Card Packs, quests, achievements and a Play row for the games.

    /hcm arcade odds

    One line per open game of chance: what it gives back and how many plays a day.

    /hcm play break

    Take a break: your own daily token limit for games of chance, or a pause of 1, 7 or 30 days.

    /hcm leave

    Leave a time-trial course or golf round. You go home with all your things.

    /hcm play accept /hcm play deny

    Answer a game invite (on Java you can click it in chat). /hcm play invites off turns every invite off.

    The fairness promises

    Games of chance give back less than you put in. These rules keep them honest about it.

    Tokens only

    No game takes or pays dollars, or takes your Cards, Minis or anything you could sell. Tokens never turn into money.

    Less back than you put in

    Each game of chance is set to give back 85 to 95 of every 100 tokens over many plays: about 87 to 90 as shipped, with the best choices in the card games. The Scratch Ticket gives back about 77. Play for fun, not to get more tokens.

    Odds first

    Before your first play, the screen shows what every result pays, how often it comes up and what the game gives back. /hcm arcade odds and this page show the same numbers.

    Decided first, then shown

    The result is saved and paid before any animation starts, and each card is saved before you see it. What stops on screen is what came up. Closing early just shows the result in chat.

    Daily limits

    Every game of chance has its own plays a day. By default they take at most 100 tokens a day between them, crates, Scratch Tickets and Card Packs bought with tokens included. The lowest limit that applies counts, and it all resets at midnight.

    “No win this time.”

    That is all a loss says: no teasing line, no play-again button, no win sound. Getting your own tokens back is never called a win.

    Take a break

    Choose how many tokens go into games of chance each day, or stop them for a while. It works even while the games are closed.

    • Your own daily limit: 10, 25, 50 or 100 tokens, or none.
    • A pause of 1, 7 or 30 days. It ends at midnight and can’t be cancelled.
    • A lower limit starts now. A higher limit, or none, waits 7 days. A pause can be made longer, never shorter.
    • It covers crates, Scratch Tickets and Card Packs bought with tokens too.
    • A grown-up can ask an admin to set a limit or a pause the player can’t lift, or to switch games of chance off for them.

    Open it with /hcm play break or the blue bed in your Wallet.

    Crates and Cards

    Crate

    15 tokens

    One prize per crate. Before you open one, the crate screen shows every prize and its chance.

    On average a crate holds less than 15 tokens’ worth at Prize Counter prices. The spin is only a show: your prize is decided before it starts.

    Rare Card

    150 tokens

    Once a week, on the Prize Counter’s Minis tab. Always Rare, Epic or Legendary, and the rarer ones come up less often. If none are left, nothing is charged.

    Card trade-in

    Free

    Spare Cards for tokens: Common 3, Uncommon 5, Rare 12, Epic 25 and Legendary 50 each. Prize Counter, Minis tab, Trade In Cards.

    Take a break covers crates. It doesn’t cover the Rare Card.
