Arcade

Tokens buy fun

Tokens are the Arcade’s coins. You can’t buy them with money and they never turn into money. You earn them just by playing.

Open it in game with /hcm arcade, or visit the Arcade in the hub.

Your Wallet

  • Tokens
  • Login streak
  • Next playtime token

In game, the Wallet also shows your last ten token changes.

How you earn them

Log in every day

Your streak pays more each day in a row: 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, then 5 a day.

Play

A token for every hour you play.

Quests

Three daily jobs and two weekly ones, different for everyone, like catching fish or visiting new biomes.

Achievements

Big moments pay once, like your first Mini or a seven-day streak.

Wild Minis

Catching one counts toward quests and achievements.

Card trade-in

Swap spare Cards for 3 to 50 tokens each. Rarer Cards give more.

Days roll over at local midnight, so streaks and daily quests reset at the same time for everyone.

The games

Crate

15 tokens

Spins and lands on a prize: boosts, hunt gear, trails, hats, filament, tokens back, or rarely a Card for any Mini.

Scratch Ticket

10 tokens

Scratch three squares. Three the same wins. The jackpot grows with every ticket anyone buys.

Rare Card

150 tokens

Once a week. A Card that’s Rare or better.

Card Packs

tokens or $

Some packs can be bought with tokens as well as dollars.

Every crate shows its chances before you open it. Animations are only a show: the result is decided and paid first, so closing early just shows the result.

The Prize Counter

Pick exactly what you want, at a fixed price.

Boosts

Speed, mining, night vision, water breathing, lucky fishing.

Hunt gear

A Mini Radar and a Mini Lure for the next wild Mini.

Cosmetics

Firework shows, particle trails and hats.

Perks

An extra home, and the /hat command.

Trophies

A numbered Arcade Trophy.

Mini supplies

Filament, a Display Case, and the weekly Rare Card.

Prizes can’t be sold for money. Shops, Pallets, vending machines, the Auction House and Courier runs all say no.
