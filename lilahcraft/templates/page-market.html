The market

Crate sells hundreds of items in eight departments, from blocks and food to tools, combat gear and redstone. Each one has real stock. Sell some in and the price drops. Buy it out and the price climbs. Sold out means nobody can buy it until somebody sells some.

                    How the market works

                    Stock sets the price

                    Plenty on the shelf means a low price; almost none left means a high one. Common items start well stocked. Diamonds and other rare items start at zero, so the first seller sets the pace.

                    The spread

                    You always sell for a little less than you'd pay to buy, so flipping a stack back and forth loses money.

                    Selling is instant, buying ships

                    Sell at any PC and get paid on the spot. Buy and pick a shipping speed; if you're offline or your inventory is full, the order waits in your Locker. Daily limits keep one player from flooding a market.

                    Hot, deals and news

                    • HOT: the price is up 8–15% for a couple of days. A good time to sell; while it lasts, everyone has a daily sell limit.
                    • DEAL: down 8–15% for a couple of days. A good time to buy, with a daily limit on buying.
                    • News Flash (UP or DOWN): a sudden jump or drop of 15–25% that fades over about a day. At most two a day, between 7 am and 9 pm Central time, and only while someone is online.
                    • WANTED: the shelf is sold out and the market wants some.
                    • Seasons: some items move 3–4% for a while. Prices also drift about 1–2% a day on their own.

                    In game: the Market News screen, the Hot & Deals sort in the store, badges on signs, holograms and display boards, and a “While you were away” catch-up when you log in.

                    Player shops: the Marketplace

                    For everything the market doesn't carry. Place a Pallet, stock it, set your price and get paid while you're offline. Shoppers find it by department on any PC.
