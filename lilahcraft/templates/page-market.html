                How the market works

                Stock sets the price

                Plenty on the shelf and it's cheap. Almost none left and it's expensive. Common stuff starts well stocked; diamonds and other rare things start at zero, so the first seller sets the pace.

                A little gap

                You always sell for a bit less than you'd pay to buy. Flipping the same stack back and forth doesn't print money.

                Selling is instant, buying ships

                Sell at any PC and get paid on the spot. Buy and pick a shipping speed; your order waits in your Locker. Daily limits keep one player from flooding a market.

                Player shops: the Marketplace

                For everything the market doesn't carry. Place a Pallet, stock it, set your price and get paid while you're offline. Shoppers find it by department on any PC.
