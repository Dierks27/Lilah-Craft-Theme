Proximity voice chat. Hear people when they’re close, lose them when they walk away.

Get it on Modrinth: Plasmo Voice

Installing mods, step by step

  1. Use a launcher that handles mods, like Prism Launcher, the CurseForge app or the Modrinth app.
  2. Make an instance on the server's Minecraft version with Fabric or NeoForge.
  3. Add the mods from the launcher, or drop the downloaded .jar files into the instance's mods folder.
  4. Launch it and join mc.lilahcraft.com.
