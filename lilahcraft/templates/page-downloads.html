Made for LilahCraft

Two client mods for this server. Both are optional and Java only, and Fabric runs both.

Collins

Version 1.5.1

Shows the TVs players put up around the server. Without it a TV is just the blocks it hangs on, and silent.

  • YouTube videos and Twitch streams, in sync for everyone in range. Those are the links players can put on a TV; staff can approve others.
  • Set your own top quality with /collinsc quality. It starts at 720.
  • Your own volume, or TVs off, from its settings screen (needs ModMenu).
  • /collins-cache clear deletes the videos it has saved.

What it does on your computer. Your game fetches each video itself, straight from YouTube or Twitch (or an address staff have approved), the way a browser would. It saves the videos in a collins-cache folder on your disk. The first time it starts, it downloads two helper programs, yt-dlp and ffmpeg. Other players with the mod see a camera symbol after your name in the tab list.

Collins is by Sawiq, Zorsh and EssciZ; this is their mod, ported to Minecraft 26.3 for LilahCraft. Free software under the GPL-3.0. Making a TV and the TV Remote are in the Guide.

  • Minecraft 26.3 only
  • Mod loader Fabric only
  • Size about 108 MB

Resource packs

The server offers two when you join, so there’s nothing to download. Both are optional: saying no changes how things look, not what works.

LilahCraft clocks

Makes clocks tell the right time in every world.

CraftBridge models

3D models for the Linked Workbench, a table on legs with its tools on a rack, and the Combo Chest, a small chest whose lid opens while it’s in use. The server’s own items get their art, where they have some. Without the pack: a plain crafting table and a plain barrel.

Said no by accident? In your server list, pick the server, click Edit, and set Server Resource Packs to Enabled.

Recommended mods

Made by other people and free. Get the version that matches your Minecraft version and loader.

JEI

Look up any recipe. On Fabric that includes the server’s own; on NeoForge, only the game’s. CraftBridge Client needs it.

Get it on Modrinth: JEI

JourneyMap

A minimap and full map. The server tells it which world you’re in, so your maps don’t get mixed up.

Get it on Modrinth: JourneyMap

Plasmo Voice

Proximity voice chat. Hear people when they’re close, lose them when they walk away.

Get it on Modrinth: Plasmo Voice

Installing mods, step by step

  1. Use a launcher that handles mods, like Prism Launcher, the CurseForge app or the Modrinth app.
  2. Make an instance on the server's Minecraft version with Fabric. NeoForge runs CraftBridge Client and JEI, but not Collins.
  3. Add the mods from the launcher, or drop the downloaded .jar files into the instance's mods folder.
  4. Launch it and join mc.lilahcraft.com.
