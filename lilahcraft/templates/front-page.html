Things to do

Survival is just the start. Everything below runs on the same dollars, tokens and Minis.

  • Trade the market

    Every raw item has real stock. Sell when the price is high, buy when it drops. Open the market

  • Collect Minis

    Numbered collectible blocks with a cap. Open packs, print Cards, hunt wild ones. Browse Minis

  • Play the Arcade

    Earn tokens just by playing. Spend them on crates, scratch tickets and prizes. Visit the Arcade

  • Run deliveries

    Grab a job on your PC, carry the parcel to a village house, get paid by distance. How deliveries work

  • Found a town

    Claim land, invite friends, set a daily tax and keep the town bank topped up. Towns and costs
