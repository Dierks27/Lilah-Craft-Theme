    How to get one

    1

    Open a Card Pack

    Buy one on a PC or in the Arcade, then right-click it. You get one Card.

    2

    Read the Card

    It says which Mini it makes and how rare it is.

    3

    Print it

    Take the Card to a Printer with some filament. Craft filament or buy it.

    4

    Your Mini!

    Numbered, graded, and sometimes Shiny. Show it off in a Display Case.

    Wild Minis

    Now and then a Mini appears in the wild near someone, and the whole server hears about it. Whoever reaches it first keeps it.

    1. Hints follow as time goes by: first the biome, then which way to go.
    2. Near the end, a light beam shows where it is.
    3. Right-click it to keep it. After about five minutes it gets away.

    A Mini Radar tells you Cold, Warm, Hot… Burning. A Mini Lure makes the next wild Mini show up near you. A Mini that gets away never uses up a number.

    Grades and Shiny

    Every print rolls a grade, shown as stars. Some come out Shiny. Rarity always beats grade.
