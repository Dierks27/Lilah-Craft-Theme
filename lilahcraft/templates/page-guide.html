The PC

Craft a PC and place it anywhere. Right-click it for its Sites: the Crate store, Sell to Crate, the Marketplace, Card Packs, the Mini Museum, Mailbox and Orders, the Courier job board and this Guide.

Buying and shipping

Order from the Crate store and pick how fast it ships. Rush costs a little more; slower is cheaper.

Your order waits in your Locker if you’re offline or your bag is full, so nothing gets lost.

Selling

Selling to Crate is instant. What you get depends on how much of that item is already in stock, so selling into a full shelf pays less.

There’s a small gap between buy and sell prices, and daily limits keep one player from flooding a market. The Market page shows live prices.

Player shops

The Marketplace is for everything the market doesn’t carry. Place a Pallet, stock it and set your price. Shoppers find it by department on any PC, and you get paid while you’re offline. The server keeps a small commission.

Mail and the Locker

Mailboxes come in eight colours. Anything you order waits in your Locker until you pick it up.

Deliveries

Grab a job from the Courier board on your PC: short runs, medium runs, and one long haul a day. You get a parcel and a destination. When you get close, a little house shows up with a villager out front. Hand them the parcel and get paid.

Pay depends on distance and how you travelled. On foot pays the most, horses and boats a bit less, elytra a lot less. Hold onto that parcel: lose it and you owe what the run was worth.

Towns

Found a town, claim land and invite friends. Claimed land is protected, so nobody can grief it. Each resident brings more land the town can claim, and you can buy extra land when you outgrow it.

Towns pay upkeep every day based on how much land they hold. Mayors set a daily tax to cover it. A town that runs out of money falls apart, so keep the bank topped up.

Crafting and storage

Linked Workbench

A crafting table that reaches into every chest within 8 blocks. With JEI, click + and it fills the grid from your chests.

Combo Chest

One block that browses every chest in range. Pull what you need, or dump your pockets and let it put things away.

Chest sorting

Double-click outside an open chest and it sorts itself. Pick a different trigger with /sort settings.

Server recipes

Some things that are hard to get here have their own recipes. They show up in your recipe book and in JEI.

Staying safe

SafeLamp

A craftable lamp that keeps hostile mobs away. Good for a front porch, great for younger players.

Graves

Die and your stuff waits in a grave where you fell. Go get it within three hours.
