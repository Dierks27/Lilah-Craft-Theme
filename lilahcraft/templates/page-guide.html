The PC

Craft a PC and place it anywhere. Right-click it for its Sites: the Crate store, Sell to Crate, the Marketplace, Card Packs, the Mini Museum, Mailbox and Orders, the Courier job board and this Guide.

Buying and shipping

Order from the Crate store and pick how fast it ships. Rush costs a little more; slower is cheaper.

If you’re offline or your inventory is full, the order waits in your Locker.

Selling

Selling to Crate is instant. What you get depends on how much of that item is already in stock, so selling into a full shelf pays less.

There’s a small gap between buy and sell prices, and daily limits keep one player from flooding a market. The Market page shows live prices.

Hot, deals and news

These tags show on signs, holograms and TVs in game, and on the Market page.

HOT
The price is up 8–15% for a couple of days: a good time to sell. While it’s HOT, everyone has a daily sell limit on it.
DEAL
The price is down 8–15% for a couple of days: a good time to buy. Buying has a daily limit.
UP or DOWN
News Flash: a sudden jump or drop of 15–25% that fades over about a day. At most two a day, between 7 am and 9 pm, and only while someone is online.
WANTED
The shelf is sold out and the market wants some.

Seasons move some items by 3–4% for a while. Prices also drift about 1–2% a day on their own.

In game: the Market News screen, the Hot & Deals sort in the store, and a “While you were away” catch-up when you log in.

Player shops

The Marketplace is for everything the market doesn’t carry. Place a Pallet, stock it and set your price. Shoppers find it by department on any PC, and you get paid while you’re offline. The server keeps a small commission.

Mail and the Locker

Mailboxes come in eight colours. An order that can’t reach you (you’re offline or your inventory is full) waits in your Locker until you pick it up.

Deliveries

Take a job from the Courier board on your PC: short runs, medium runs, and one long haul a day. You get a parcel and a destination. Near it, a small house appears with a villager out front. Hand over the parcel to get paid.

Pay depends on distance and how you travelled. On foot pays the most, horses and boats a bit less, elytra a lot less. Lose the parcel and you owe what the run was worth.

Towns

Found a town, claim land and invite players. Claimed land is protected: nobody can grief it. Each resident adds to the land the town can claim, and you can buy extra land when you outgrow it.

Towns pay upkeep every day based on how much land they hold, and mayors set a daily tax to cover it. A town that runs out of money falls apart. Keep the bank topped up.

Crafting and storage

Linked Workbench

A crafting table that reaches into every chest within 8 blocks. With JEI, click + and it fills the grid from your chests.

Combo Chest

One block that browses every chest in range. Pull what you need, or empty your inventory into it and it puts things away.

Chest sorting

Double-click outside an open chest and it sorts itself. Pick a different trigger with /sort settings.

Server recipes

Some things that are hard to get here have their own recipes. They show up in your recipe book and in JEI.

Golem chests

Copper golems, hopper filters and item sorters decide where things go by what a slot already holds. If the Linked Workbench, the Combo Chest or the CraftBridge Client’s storage panel took the last item out of such a slot, the sorting would quietly stop working.

A golem chest is a chest, double chest or barrel that CraftBridge never takes the last item of any slot from. Storage always leaves one of each item behind, and the golems keep sorting into it.

Craft the Golem Chest Marker, hold it in your main hand and right-click a chest, double chest or barrel to mark it; right-click again to unmark it. You get a chat line and a sound, and the chest doesn’t open. A double chest is marked on both halves.

While you hold the marker, marked containers within 16 blocks sparkle. Only you see it.

Sound Muffler

A block that hushes the sounds made around it. Place it and its menu opens; right-click it any time to change things.

Pick what to hush: a whole group (farm animals, pets, villagers, golems, monsters, other players, footsteps, fishing, pistons, doors and gates, bells, chests and more) or any single sound. Each click goes Normal → Quieter → Silent; right-click steps back.

Heard nearby lists every sound made in its area in the last 10 minutes: stand by the noisy farm, open it, click the culprit. Find a sound: type part of a name, like chicken or piston. A single sound can override its group, so villagers can be Silent while their trading sound is set to “Always play”.

It hushes sounds made inside its box for everyone, wherever they stand. Anyone can look at its menu. Break it and the item remembers its settings.

Some sounds can’t be hushed. They come from each player’s own game: rain and thunder, music and jukeboxes, furnaces and campfires crackling, portals, lava, minecarts rolling, bees in flight, and your own footsteps. Rule of thumb: anything in Heard nearby can be hushed.

Staying safe

SafeLamp

A craftable lamp that keeps hostile mobs away. Good for a front porch.

Graves

When you die, your things wait in a grave where you fell. You have three hours to get them back.
