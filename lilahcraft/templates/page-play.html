Mods that make it better

None are required, and they're all Java only.

Get them on the Downloads page

JEI

Look up any recipe, including the server’s own. Click + and the Linked Workbench fills the grid from your chests.

JourneyMap

A minimap and full map. The server tells it which world you’re in, so your maps don’t get mixed up.

Plasmo Voice

Proximity voice chat. Hear people when they’re close, lose them when they walk away.

CraftBridge Client

Lets the Combo Chest and Linked Workbench show every item in range, not just the first few dozen.

For parents

LilahCraft is a family server, run out of a house in Minnesota. Here’s what’s good to know.

Everyone plays together

Java and Bedrock players join the same server and play in the same worlds, on a computer, a phone, a tablet, a Switch or an Xbox.

Claimed land is protected

Land a town has claimed is protected, so nobody can grief it (Minecraft slang for wrecking someone else’s build).

Gentle on younger players

A craftable SafeLamp keeps hostile mobs away. And if you die, your things wait in a grave for three hours.

Tokens aren’t for sale

Arcade tokens can’t be bought with money and never turn into money, and prizes can’t be sold for money.

Voice chat is optional

It only exists through an optional Java mod, Plasmo Voice, and you only hear people standing close by.

Nothing to install

Mods are optional, and Bedrock players need nothing installed.
