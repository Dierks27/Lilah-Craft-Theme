The worlds

The Hub

Where you spawn. Always midnight, no building, just portals, the Arcade and a nice view.

Lilah’s World

The main survival world. Mobs are on, so bring a sword. It has its own Nether and End, and your inventory travels with you between them.

Say yes to the resource pack

When you join, the server offers a small resource pack. It's what makes clocks tell the right time in every world.

Coming soon: CraftBridge models

Mods that make it better

None are required, and they're all Java only.

Get them on the Downloads page

JEI

Look up any recipe, including the server’s own. Click + and the Linked Workbench fills the grid from your chests.

JourneyMap

A minimap and full map. The server tells it which world you’re in, so your maps don’t get mixed up.

Plasmo Voice

Proximity voice chat. Hear people when they’re close, lose them when they walk away.

CraftBridge Client

Lets the Combo Chest and Linked Workbench show every item in range, not just the first few dozen.
